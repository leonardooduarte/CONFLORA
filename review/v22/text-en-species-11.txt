Botanical profile

Yellow flowers in umbels; forms small colonies. The source records it as a Black Swallowtail host plant.

Cultivation in the source context

The source describes full to partial sun, summer-dry tolerance and low maintenance in North America. Its height differs from the Iowa guide; ranges were not artificially merged.
