01 / INVESTIGATE
Rain garden

A vegetated depression receives runoff and may support retention and infiltration when designed for the site.

Before choosing

Check soil, setbacks, contributing area, overflow and utilities. Not an automatic fix for an already flooded spot.

Follow up

Observe sediment, inlet/outlet and plants; keep post-rain records.

02 / INVESTIGATE
Vegetated area

Ground cover and roots participate in water–surface relationships.

Before choosing

Check climate, light, soil, space and plant origin. Vegetation does not remove flood risk.

Follow up

Observe establishment, unwanted plants and locally appropriate watering.

03 / INVESTIGATE
Permeable surface

A surface-and-base system allows water passage under designed conditions.

Before choosing

Check loading, base, soil, contamination and maintenance. A drawing does not prove permeability.

Follow up

Investigate clogging and maintenance with technical support.
